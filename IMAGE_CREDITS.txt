IMAGE & MEDIA CREDITS
=====================

All bundled photographs and videos in this template are 
AI-generated using Kimi AI, Grok AI, and Runway AI, then 
provided as demonstration content only.

These demo files are intended to give buyers a complete preview 
of how the template looks fully populated. Buyers should replace 
all bundled media with their own photography or properly 
licensed assets before deploying the template to a live website.

The audio folder ships empty — see audio/README.txt for 
guidance on adding your own royalty-free tracks.
