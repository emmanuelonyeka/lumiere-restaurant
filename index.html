Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Nestled in the heart of the city, Lumière has been a beacon of gastronomic innovation since 1987. Our philosophy is simple: to create extraordinary dining experiences that awaken the senses and linger in memory.

Under the guidance of Executive Chef Antoine Moreau, each dish is a masterpiece crafted with the finest seasonal ingredients, sourced from local artisans and trusted purveyors around the world.

Your Evening

What to Expect

Everything you need to know before your visit — so you arrive with nothing but anticipation

01

Arrival & Welcome

Our team greets you at the entrance from the moment you arrive. Valet parking is available on Avenue des Champs-Élysées. We recommend arriving 10 minutes before your reservation to enjoy a welcome aperitif in our lounge.

02

The Experience

The Chef's Tasting Menu unfolds across 12 courses over approximately 3 to 3.5 hours — an unhurried journey, never rushed. À la Carte dining typically spans 2 hours. Wine pairings are curated tableside by our sommelier.

03

Your Departure

We present mignardises and your personalised menu card to take home as a keepsake. Your valet is summoned as you finish. Payment is settled discreetly at the table — cards and digital payment accepted.

Dress Code

Smart elegant attire is kindly requested. We ask that gentlemen wear a jacket. Sportswear and casual footwear are respectfully not permitted.

Reservations

Advance booking is essential. We hold your table for 15 minutes past the reservation time. For parties of 6 or more, a credit card guarantee is required at the time of booking.

Parking & Access

Complimentary valet service is available at our entrance. The nearest Métro station is George V (Line 1), a 3-minute walk. We are fully wheelchair accessible.

Dietary Requirements

Please inform us of any allergies or dietary needs at the time of booking. Our kitchen accommodates vegetarian, vegan, and allergen-free menus with advance notice of 48 hours.

Duration

The Tasting Menu is a 3 to 3.5 hour experience. À la Carte dining is approximately 2 hours. Private Dining events are tailored to your schedule. We never rush an evening at Lumière.

Children & Occasions

We welcome guests aged 12 and above for evening service. For celebrations — anniversaries, proposals, birthdays — please note this at booking and our team will prepare something special.

Still have questions? Our reservations team is delighted to assist.
